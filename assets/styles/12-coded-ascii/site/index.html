From cherry to cup

Every coffee cherry holds two seeds. Scroll to follow them to the cup.

01 — The cherry

A coffee cherry is a fruit. Split one open and you will usually find two seeds, flat faces pressed together.

02 — The seed

Pulped, washed and dried in the sun, the seeds become green coffee.

03 — The roast

Heat turns them brown. Around first crack they pop, loud enough to hear.

04 — The grind

Ground just before brewing, so the aroma stays in the cup.

05 — The cup

Espresso: hot water pushed through the grounds at about 9 bar.
